About Jagruti Patrika: Jagruti Patrika is an independent literary community dedicated to Odia and bilingual poetry, short stories, articles, essays, and literary discussion (and is not affiliated with any government authority). By creating an account or signing in to the Jagruti Patrika Android app, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate your account, display your author profile, publish literary works, support community interactions, and maintain platform safety:

a) Account & Registration Information

  • Full name / display name, optional Odia script pen name, unique @username, author role, optional bio, and optional city or region.
  • Email address, email verification status, Firebase Authentication User ID (UID), and linked sign-in provider identifiers (google.com or password).

b) Profile Picture & Default Avatar Selection

  • Optional Google profile picture URL (when signing in with Google), optional custom uploaded JPEG profile portrait, or your selected built-in default avatar preference (male, female, or literary initials fallback).

c) Literary Content & Community Activity

  • Text-only literary posts (poems, stories, articles, reflections), titles, categories, language tags (Odia, English, Bilingual), and reading-time estimates.
  • Comments, post appreciations (likes), reading list bookmarks, follower/following connections, and blocked user records.
  • One-to-one text-only direct messages between authors, conversation metadata, unread counters, and read timestamps.
  • Content moderation reports and user safety reports submitted by you.

d) Notifications, Legal Consent & Technical Information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token, generated device ID, Android manufacturer/model label, push notification toggle preferences, and Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS runtime permission status (Android 13+).
  • Immutable legal consent records (your UID, accepted policy versions, consent screen version, and server acceptance timestamps).
  • Standard network/transport telemetry processed by Google Firebase SDKs to authenticate sessions and deliver database updates or push notifications.
Permissions We Do Not Request: Jagruti Patrika does not request broad device storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) and does not access your contacts, microphone, camera feed, or precise location.

2. Google Sign-In and Email Authentication

You may register or sign in using Google Sign-In (via Android Credential Manager / Google Identity) or Email and Password authentication, and you may link both methods to the same Firebase UID.

  • Password Handling: When you use Email and Password authentication, your password is handled exclusively by Firebase Authentication. Jagruti Patrika never stores passwords or password hashes in Cloud Firestore.
  • Private Account Metadata: Your email address, email verification status, and linked sign-in provider list are stored in a private user subcollection accessible only to your authenticated UID.
  • No Anonymous Browsing: Access to community feeds and profiles requires signing in with an authenticated account.

3. Profile Information and Profile Pictures

Profile photographs are strictly optional across all sign-in methods.

  • Built-In Default Avatars: The app bundles local Male and Female vector avatars as well as a literary initials monogram. Default avatars stay bundled on your device and are never uploaded to Firebase Storage; only your preference (defaultAvatarType) is saved in your Firestore profile document. Avatar choice is never inferred from your name, email address, or Google profile.
  • Google Account Photos: If you sign in with Google and have a Google profile photo, its URL may be linked directly in your profile without downloading or copying the image to Firebase Storage unless you explicitly upload a custom photo.
  • Custom Profile Photo Uploads: When selected via Android's system Photo Picker, custom images are validated (JPEG, PNG, or WebP, maximum 5 MB pre-compression), cropped, and compressed to 400 × 400 JPEG before upload to your UID-scoped Firebase Storage path. Removing a custom portrait deletes the stored custom file and restores your Google photo (if available) or your selected default avatar.
  • Public vs. Private Profile Fields: Your display name, Odia pen name, @username, role, bio, location, active profile avatar, and verification badge status are visible to other signed-in members. Your email address is kept private.

4. User-Submitted Literary Content

  • Text-Only Literary Policy: Literary posts and direct messages are text-only (user post image uploads, videos, reels, audio, and GIF attachments are disabled). Legacy posts created prior to this policy may remain preserved in Firestore.
  • Author Ownership: You retain full ownership and copyright of original poems, stories, articles, reflections, and comments you submit.
  • Visibility & Moderation: Published works and comments are visible to authenticated community members. Reports submitted by users and moderation actions taken by authorized Editorial Administrators are logged for community safety and copyright compliance.

5. Firebase and Third-Party Services

Jagruti Patrika relies on Google Firebase infrastructure and Android platform services to operate:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages user sign-in, email verification, password resets, and session tokens.
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores author profiles, private email/provider records, literary posts, comments, follows, bookmarks, direct messages, notifications, consent records, and moderation records.
  • Firebase Storage: Stores optional user-uploaded custom profile portraits under owner-scoped paths.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers optional push notifications for editorial announcements when enabled.
  • Android Credential Manager / Google Sign-In: Facilitates Google account authentication.
  • Local Image Caching (Coil): Caches viewed avatar images locally in app memory and disk cache (jagruti_avatar_cache) to reduce repeated network downloads.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

  • Infrastructure Processor: Data is processed by Google LLC (Firebase services) solely to host, authenticate, synchronize, and deliver app functionality.
  • Community Visibility: Public profile fields, published literary posts, and comments are visible to authenticated users of Jagruti Patrika; direct messages are accessible only to the two conversation participants.
  • Legal & Safety Obligations: Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to investigate fraud, abuse, or copyright infringement.
